Yes, this is possible. You keep syncing the Air through the Google Health app. OpenAir reads everything back through the Google Health API v4 (HRV, sleep stages, SpO₂, resting HR, skin temperature, intraday heart rate) and gets a webhook when new data lands, so updates and alerts arrive near real time.

The catch: no public API exposes a Stress, Readiness or Sleep score. OpenFit doesn't show one either. We'll compute our own Stress Load and Recovery from HRV, resting HR, sleep and intraday HR, label them as estimates, and show what goes into each.

Architecture

The recommended setup is an installable web app (PWA) plus a small backend. That's better than "a website with a link": it gets a home-screen icon, full-screen launch, offline cache and push notifications, and the server keeps syncing while your phone is locked.

Sync strategy

  1. Backfill 90 days on first connect, rate-limited.
  2. Webhook: Google pings us, we fetch only the changed interval.
  3. Cron safety net every 30–60 min.
  4. Sync now button, plus a link that opens Google Health to make the band sync.

What "auto sync" can't do

Nothing outside Google's app can trigger the band → phone sync. The Google Health app does that in the background, or right away when you open it. Once the data reaches Google's cloud, OpenAir picks it up within minutes on its own.

OAuth for personal use

In "Testing" mode, refresh tokens expire every 7 days. Switching the consent screen to "In production" while unverified keeps tokens alive (100-user cap, "unverified app" warning). That's fine for one person.

Stress & recovery (our estimates)

Stress Load · 0–100 daily

HRV below your 28-day baseline, resting HR above baseline, sleep debt, and minutes of elevated HR while sitting still.

Stress moments · intraday

HR ≥ resting + ~22 bpm for 10+ min with almost no steps and no workout. You can tag each one (meeting, coffee, commute) to find patterns.

Recovery · 0–100 each morning

HRV 40%, resting HR 25%, sleep vs goal 20%, respiration and skin-temp deviation 15%. Shows "learning" until there are 7+ nights of data.

Illness watch

Two or more of: RHR ↑, respiration ↑, skin temp ↑, HRV ↓, two nights running. Flagged as a heads-up, not a diagnosis.

Roadmap

Phase 01–2 days
Spike. Google Cloud project, OAuth client, a probe script for every data type, a test webhook.
Exit: real HRV, sleep, SpO₂ and RHR for your Air; webhook fires after a phone sync; token outlives 7 days.
Phase 1
Sync foundation. Next.js app, Google sign-in, Postgres schema, encrypted tokens, backfill, webhook, cron, sync screen.
Exit: 90 days in the DB; new data shows within minutes of a phone sync.
Phase 2
Dashboards. Today, Sleep, Heart & HRV, Activity, Trends; PWA install and offline cache.
Exit: installed on your phone, usable one-handed.
Phase 3
Derived metrics. Baselines, Stress Load, stress moments, Recovery, illness watch.
Exit: every score explains itself; backtest on history looks sane.
Phase 4
Alerts. Web Push, rules engine, quiet hours, morning brief, history.
Exit: morning brief arrives on your phone.
Phase 5
Extras. AI coach, export, Capacitor app with widgets, journaling tags for correlations.
